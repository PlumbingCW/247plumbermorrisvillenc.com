Plumber in Breckenridge, Morrisville, NC.

Breckenridge is one of the larger named residential areas in Morrisville, and the Town's own lists split it into many small sections. That matters when something leaks, because a single-family house and a townhome in the same neighborhood do not share the same first steps.

If water is running where it should not, close the main shutoff and call (919) 944-7281. Tell dispatch which Breckenridge section or street you are on.

Which part of Breckenridge are you in?

The Town of Morrisville lists a "Breckenridge Area" in its first residency district, and its trash route lists name several Breckenridge sections one by one. We have not found a published map of exact boundaries, so we describe the area by the section names the Town uses and by the streets those lists attach to them.

The table below shows what the Town's lists say. It is a quick way to work out whether your home is a detached house or an attached townhome before you call.

Detached homes and townhomes call for different first steps.

In a detached house you own the supply line from the meter to the water heater and every fixture on it. The shutoff is usually where the line enters the house, often in a garage, utility closet or crawlspace. Close it, switch off power near any water and then call.

In a townhome the picture can change. A leak may start in a wall you share with a neighbor, and your association may have rules about who repairs what and who carries insurance for it. The Town says many Morrisville neighborhoods have homeowners associations and that covenants can set standards stricter than Town code, so read yours before assuming the repair is yours alone.

Water heaters and drains in Breckenridge homes.

A neighborhood data site reports that most homes in the Breckenridge area were built in 2000 or later. We did not check build years against county records, so treat that as a rough guide. It does mean many water heaters here have been running a long time, and the rating label on the tank shows the manufacture date.

A tank that leaks from the body cannot be patched, so water heater replacement is the usual answer. If you have hot water that runs out quickly or turns lukewarm, water heater repair is worth checking first. Slow or repeat clogs in a kitchen or bathroom line are a job for drain cleaning, and a gurgling main line points to sewer line cleaning.

Water and sewer service in Breckenridge.

The Town of Morrisville says water and sewer service for its residents is provided by the Town of Cary. If the whole street loses pressure or sewage backs up in the street, call Cary at (919) 469-4000 and tell them you are outside Cary town limits. If the problem is inside your home, it is a plumbing call.

Permits and inspections for Morrisville addresses come from the Town of Morrisville Inspections Department at 919-463-6190, 100 Town Hall Drive. Ask before a water heater swap that changes fuel, location or venting.

Sources: Town of Morrisville new residents page, Town residential inspections page.
