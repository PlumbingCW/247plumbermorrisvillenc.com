Where Providence Place sits in the Town.

The Town's lists show Providence Place as single-family homes, with Falcon Ridge listed as single-family and townhomes. Streets the Town attaches to Providence Place include Mason Farm Road and Mayo Forest Lane. Mason Farms appears as its own entry, so check which name your street carries.

A neighborhood data site that treats Providence Place together with nearby Shiloh Grove reports that most homes there were built after 1999. We did not verify that against county records, so use it as a rough guide.

Choosing a water heater: tank, heat pump or tankless.

In a newer neighborhood the question is often what to put in when the first water heater wears out. A tank unit is the familiar choice. A heat pump model uses electricity more efficiently, and a tankless unit heats on demand. Each has its own venting, electrical or gas needs, so the choice depends on what your home already has.

Our water heater installation page walks through those options, and tankless water heater service covers flushing and repair. Duke Energy Progress supplies electricity to Morrisville and Enbridge Gas supplies natural gas, so check your own utility's rebate pages before buying, because offers change.

Questions to settle before a replacement.

Five answers make a quote easier to compare. Does the new unit match your current fuel? Does the vent route stay the same? Is there room for the new tank or a wall-mounted unit? Does the old one need a drain pan or an expansion tank? And who pulls the permit if one is needed?

Ask the plumber to put the answers in writing. If anyone cannot explain the permit question, call the Town Inspections Department before agreeing to the work.
