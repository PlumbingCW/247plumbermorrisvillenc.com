Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 30, 2026. This policy explains what information Morrisville 24/7 Plumbing collects when you use this website or call our line.

Calls to our line.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and a description of the plumbing problem so we can dispatch help.

This website.

This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information through the site other than what your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address and browser type, in the server logs of our hosting provider.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps through Google Maps embeds. Google may set cookies or collect usage data under its own privacy policy when those load.

How we use information.

We use the information you give us to answer your call, send a plumber, confirm the job and follow up on service. We share it with the licensed plumbers who perform the work and as the law requires.

Your choices.

You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@247plumbermorrisvillenc.com. You can tell the dispatcher you do not want a call recorded, and we will tell you if we cannot dispatch without it.
