Plumber in Town Hall Commons, Morrisville, NC.

Town Hall Commons is a group of named sections in Morrisville that mixes single-family homes with townhomes. The Town's lists name the Gardens, Village, Gables, Terraces, Birkshires and Town Hall North sections separately.

That mix shapes a plumbing call. Say which section you are in, close your main shutoff if water is moving and call (919) 944-7281.

Townhomes: shared walls and shared consequences.

When your home shares a wall, floor or ceiling, a leak can reach a neighbor within minutes. Close your main shutoff at once and keep it closed until the source is found. Photograph the damage before anyone cleans up, because your insurer and your association will both ask for it.

If a drain backs up in an attached home, stop using every fixture. A blocked branch line serving only your unit is a drain cleaning job, but a blockage that affects several units points to a shared line and an association or property manager. Describe exactly which fixtures are slow so dispatch can help sort out which it is.

Permits and inspections come from the Town.

The Town of Morrisville Inspections Department handles plumbing permits and inspections at 100 Town Hall Drive, phone 919-463-6190. Its page says a permit is required to install, extend, relocate or replace a plumbing system, while minor fixture repairs that do not disturb supply, waste or vent piping may be exempt. The code behind those permits is the 2024 North Carolina Plumbing Code (based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code, IPC), mandatory since July 1, 2025.

For water heater installation, ask the Town before work starts if the new unit changes the fuel, the location or the venting. In a townhome, also ask your association whether it has its own approval process.

Leak in an attached home?

Close the shutoff, switch off power near the water and call the line. Tell dispatch whether neighbors share the wall.

Common calls from attached and single-family homes.

Fixture trouble is the everyday case in any neighborhood. A running toilet, a dripping faucet or a leaking supply valve can usually be handled by toilet repair or faucet and sink repair. If your bill rises with no change in habits, leak detection checks whether water is escaping unseen.

Not every problem is yours to fix. The Town of Cary provides water and sewer service for Morrisville, so a street-wide pressure loss or a sewer problem in the road is a call to Cary at (919) 469-4000.
