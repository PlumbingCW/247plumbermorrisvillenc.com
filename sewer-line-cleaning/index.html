Who clears which part of the sewer line in Morrisville.

The cleanout sets the dividing line.

Morrisville's sewer service comes from the Town of Cary. Cary says it clears blockages in the main sewer line and in the lateral up to the cleanout, which is a capped, vertical 4 inch pipe at the edge of your property. You are responsible for the lateral between that cleanout and your home. If there is no cleanout at the edge, you are responsible for blockages anywhere in the lateral.

Cary lists sewer blockage help on weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. at 311 or (919) 469-4090, and after hours and holidays at (919) 469-4012. Cary's 311 line is for callers inside Cary, so from Morrisville the ten digit numbers are the safer choice. Morrisville's resident guide says Cary Public Works handles emergency sewer service.

Source: Town of Cary, sewer lateral and cleanout guidance. Morrisville A to Z guide.

Signs the main sewer line is blocked.

A blocked main shows up in more than one place. The lowest drain in the house is usually first, because sewage rises to the lowest opening. A toilet that gurgles when the washing machine drains is another early sign, and so is water that lingers in a shower after the toilet flushes.

Check these before you call.

  • Two or more fixtures slow or backing up at once, rather than one sink.
  • Gurgling from a toilet or a floor drain when another fixture drains.
  • Water or sewage in the lowest tub, shower or basement-level floor drain.
  • A sewer smell in the bathrooms or yard near the cleanout.
  • Slow drains returning within days of a cable visit.

Finding and opening your cleanout safely.

Most cleanouts are a short capped pipe near the foundation or at the property edge. Here is how to approach one.

  1. 01

    Look for the cap.

    Check along the front wall of the house and near the yard edge for a round plastic or metal cap, usually 3 or 4 inches wide, flush or slightly raised.

    .
  2. 02

    Stand to the side.

    Never put your face above the cap. A full line can push sewage out the moment the cap loosens.

    .
  3. 03

    Loosen slowly.

    Use a wrench or large pliers and turn the cap a quarter turn. If sewage seeps up, tighten it again.

    .
  4. 04

    Note what you see.

    A dry pipe or standing sewage tells us, and Cary, where the blockage sits. Then close it and call.

    .

How we clear a main line blockage.

We start at the cleanout whenever there is one. A sectional or drum machine carries a cable with a cutting head through the lateral. Cutters chew roots and grease, and a retrieving head can grab a toy, rag or wipe wad. We pull back slowly and watch what comes out.

A clog at a single sink or tub is a branch-line job, and drain cleaning covers it. Only the shared main needs this larger equipment.

After the line is open, we flush with water to confirm flow and, where the blockage was bad or has happened before, we send a camera in. The picture tells us whether the cause is roots, a sag, a bad joint or a one-off item.

Sewer drain cleaning and the blockages that return.

Some blockages come back within weeks. That points to roots in a joint, a belly that holds water, or grease and wipes sticking to rough pipe. When the cause is film on the wall we recommend hydro jetting, which washes the entire wall rather than punching a hole through it.

If the camera shows a crack or collapse, cleaning is only a pause. Our sewer line repair page covers lining, spot repair and replacement.

What the code and Cary say about your side of the pipe.

Clearing a blockage is maintenance, not new construction, so the repair code matters less than the property line. Cary treats the lateral from the cleanout to your house as the owner's side, and any later pipe replacement falls under the 2024 North Carolina Plumbing Code, based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).

If a camera shows damage, that is the point where permits and licensing come into play, and the Inspections Department at 919-463-6190 can say what applies.

What changes the price of sewer line cleaning.

Clearing a main line is priced after we find the access point and the cause. Several things move the figure.

Access.

A cleanout we can open is simple. A line reached through a roof vent or by pulling a toilet takes more time.

Length of the lateral.

A short line is quick, while a long run needs more cable and more passes.

What the blockage is.

A paper plug clears differently from a root mass or a hard grease layer.

Camera follow-up.

A recording after cleaning adds time and gives you evidence of the pipe condition.

Which side of the cleanout.

If the blockage proves to be in the town main, that part is the Town's to clear.

Insurance rarely covers the clearing itself. Cleanup after a sudden sewer backup is covered only with a backup endorsement on your policy, so check yours. You get a clear price before work starts.
