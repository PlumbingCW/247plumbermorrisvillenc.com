Contact Morrisville 24/7 Plumbing.

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. The line is open 24/7 for emergencies and scheduled plumbing work in Morrisville, NC.

Call (919) 944-7281

Call dispatch.

Call (919) 944-7281 any time. We do not use contact forms, because a plumbing problem needs a conversation, not a ticket.

Our email is contact@247plumbermorrisvillenc.com for non-urgent questions. For anything involving water, call.

What to tell dispatch.

Have these ready.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water or smell is coming from, and whether you have shut it off.
  • Whether anyone is at risk, such as a gas smell or water near electrical outlets.
  • Who can give access if you are not home, such as a tenant or property manager.

Utility numbers worth saving.

Not every problem is ours to fix. Cary provides water and sewer service for Morrisville and handles main line blockages up to your cleanout. Cary's public pages list 311 inside Cary and (919) 469-4000 for callers outside town limits, which includes Morrisville.

For a gas smell, leave the building and call Enbridge Gas at 1-877-776-2427 or 911 from a safe distance. For digging, dial 811. Our gas line page explains the full steps.

What happens after you call.

The call is the start of the job, so here is what to expect.

  1. 01

    A dispatcher answers.

    A person takes your address and asks what you can see, hear or smell.

    .
  2. 02

    Emergencies are sorted first.

    Water that will not stop and sewage in the house go ahead of routine work. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

    .
  3. 03

    You get a window, not a promise.

    Dispatch gives a realistic arrival window for your address. Traffic on I-40, NC 54 and NC 147 can change it.

  4. 04

    The plumber explains before working.

    The plumber finds the cause and gives a clear price before work starts. You can say no.

Before you call: a short safety checklist.

Do these while you dial.

  • Close the main water shutoff if water is still flowing.
  • Do not touch electrical outlets or panels if you are standing in water.
  • Do not use drains that are backing up with sewage.
  • Move valuables and electronics off wet floors.
  • Take photos for your insurer before cleanup.

Non-urgent questions and business inquiries.

For a quote on planned work, such as a water heater install or a repipe, call the same number and say it is not urgent. Dispatch will book a same-day appointment when available or the next opening.

Property managers and landlords can send repair requests by phone with the unit number, the tenant's contact and any access instructions. Email works for invoices and records at contact@247plumbermorrisvillenc.com, but not for anything involving water on the floor.
