How we choose what to write about.

Every guide answers a question homeowners actually type into a search box, such as what a repair costs, who is allowed to do the work or how to tell a quick fix from a real problem. We then check local facts against the Town of Morrisville, the Town of Cary, Enbridge Gas and North Carolina sources, and we name those sources on the page.

We do not publish prices we cannot support. Where a cost depends on the home, the guides list what changes it. If a guide leaves you unsure, call (919) 944-7281 and dispatch will tell you whether it is an emergency, and our plumbing services pages explain each repair in more depth.

Where to start in an emergency.

These guides are for planning, not for an active leak. If water is flowing, close the main shutoff and call. If you smell gas, leave and call Enbridge Gas at 1-877-776-2427 or 911 from a safe distance before you phone anyone else.
