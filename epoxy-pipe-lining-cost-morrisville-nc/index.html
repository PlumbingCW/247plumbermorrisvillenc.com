Epoxy Pipe Lining Cost in Morrisville, NC: What Sets the Price.

Epoxy pipe lining cost in Morrisville, NC comes down to five things. These are the length of the run, the pipe width, the number of access points, the cleaning the old pipe needs, and whether a camera shows the pipe can hold a liner at all. No honest figure exists before that camera look, so this page explains the drivers instead of guessing.

If a drain is backing up today, call (919) 944-7281 first. Lining is planned work, and every lining job starts with clearing the line and running a camera through it.

By the Morrisville 24/7 Plumbing team · Published 2026-11-07

What you are paying for with a lined pipe.

Epoxy lining puts a resin-soaked felt or fiberglass sleeve inside an existing pipe, inflates it against the wall, and lets it harden into a smooth new pipe within the old one. The old pipe stays in the ground, so the trench is replaced by one or two small access holes.

That sounds simple, and the cure itself is. Most of the price sits in the steps before it: cleaning, inspection, access, and preparing the resin. Pricing a lining job without seeing the inside of the pipe is guesswork.

Why the camera report decides everything.

A camera head on a push cable shows the pipe wall, the joints, and any sag where water pools. It also gives the crew a footage count, which is the number a lining quote should be built on. If someone gives you a firm price without a recording, ask what they based it on.

Ask for the recording or still images, and ask where on the pipe each problem sits. That record is useful whether you line, spot repair, or replace. A separate sewer camera inspection is a reasonable first step when you are comparing quotes.

Morrisville note: where your responsibility for the sewer pipe starts.

Morrisville sewer service comes from the Town of Cary. Cary says it clears blockages in the main and in the lateral up to the cleanout, a capped vertical 4 inch pipe at the property edge. The owner is responsible for the lateral between the cleanout and the house.

If there is no cleanout at the edge of the property, the owner is responsible for blockages anywhere in the lateral. That means a lining quote only concerns the owner's section. Before you pay for any lining, find your cleanout and confirm where your section begins.

Pipes that lining cannot fix.

Warning signs that point away from lining.

A liner needs a pipe that is still a pipe. These findings usually send a job toward spot repair or replacement.

  • A collapsed or crushed section that a camera cannot pass.
  • A belly or sag that holds standing water, since a liner follows the existing shape.
  • A joint that has separated badly enough to leave a gap or a shifted offset.
  • Heavy root intrusion that cannot be cut back to the pipe wall.
  • A pipe that is not a straight, accessible run, such as a line under a slab with no way in.

None of these mean the pipe is hopeless. They mean the cheapest-looking option may not be the right one. Read about sewer line repair options before deciding, and ask the contractor to explain why lining fits your pipe in particular.

The diameter point surprises people. A liner has thickness, so the bore shrinks a little. On a long run with many branches that tradeoff is normally acceptable, but it is why a contractor should confirm the finished size on a short pipe.

Questions to ask before you sign.

Put these to every contractor.

  • Does the price include pre-cleaning and the final camera recording?
  • Is the crew a licensed plumbing contractor? You can check the NC licensing board search at nclicensing.org.
  • What is the cure method, and how long must the line stay out of service?
  • How many branches will be reopened, and is that in the price?
  • What happens if the camera finds a collapse after work starts?
  • Does the workmanship warranty cover the liner and the reinstated connections in writing?

Standards that apply to a lined pipe.
