Terms of Service.

Last updated October 30, 2026. These terms describe how our dispatch line and website work.

How the service works.

You call our line, a dispatcher takes the details and a licensed and insured plumber is sent. The plumber confirms the cause of the problem and states a clear price before work starts. You decide whether to proceed.

Calls and recording.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By calling you agree to this.

Arrival and availability.

Dispatch gives an estimated arrival window based on conditions at the time. Traffic, weather and job volume can change it. We do not guarantee a fixed arrival time, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

Website content.

The pages on this site are general information about plumbing and are not a substitute for an on-site diagnosis. Local utility numbers and permit rules can change, so confirm them with the Town of Morrisville, the Town of Cary or the utility named.

Limits of liability.

To the extent the law allows, our liability for information on this website is limited to what the law requires. Work performed on your property is covered by the terms the plumber gives you in writing or on the invoice.

Contact.

Questions about these terms can go to contact@247plumbermorrisvillenc.com or (919) 944-7281.
