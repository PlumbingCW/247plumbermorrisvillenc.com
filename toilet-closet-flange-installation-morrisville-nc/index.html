Toilet Closet Flange Installation in Morrisville: Height, Repair and When to Call.

A toilet closet flange is the ring on the floor that anchors the toilet and connects its outlet to the drain. A good installation sits on top of the finished floor, is bolted to the subfloor, and holds a wax ring seal under the toilet without any rocking.

If your toilet wobbles, leaks at the base, or you see a cracked ring when you pull it, the flange is the part to look at. Here is how a flange is set properly in Morrisville, NC homes and where a do-it-yourself fix stops being a good idea.

By the Morrisville 24/7 Plumbing team · Published 2026-11-07

Signs the flange is the problem.

Check these before you replace the toilet.

  • The toilet rocks even after you tighten the closet bolts.
  • Water or a stain appears around the base after flushing.
  • A sewer smell comes from the bathroom floor and the bowl is dry.
  • The floor around the toilet feels soft or spongy underfoot.
  • The ring is cracked, rusted through or the bolt slots have snapped.

A leak at the base can also come from a worn wax ring or a loose bolt. The only way to tell is to lift the toilet and look at the flange itself. If the floor is soft, stop using the toilet and call, because the subfloor may need repair before any flange is set.

Where the flange belongs.

The flange should sit on top of the finished floor surface, not below it. When tile or new flooring goes in after the flange was set, the ring can end up low, and the wax ring then cannot seal. The usual fix is a spacer or an extension ring, or a flange reset at the correct height.

The center of the drain opening is usually about 12 inches from the finished wall, which is the standard rough-in for most toilets. Check the box for your toilet, because a 10 inch or 14 inch model needs a different spot.

Materials, and why they matter.

Flanges come in PVC, ABS, cast iron and brass, and stainless repair rings exist for flanges with broken bolt slots. The ring has to match the drain pipe material and size, typically a 3 inch or 4 inch connection. Gluing the wrong plastic to the wrong pipe is a failure that shows up months later.

A repair ring is a legitimate fix when the pipe below is sound and the flange is only cracked. A new flange cut into the drain is the better answer when the pipe itself is damaged, which is where a camera or a look from the crawlspace helps.

What a toilet flange change means for permits.

North Carolina uses the 2024 North Carolina Plumbing Code, which became mandatory on July 1, 2025 and is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code with state amendments.

The Town of Morrisville says a plumbing permit is required for installation, extension, relocation or replacement of a plumbing system, while minor fixture repairs that do not disrupt water supply, waste or vent may be exempt. Resetting a toilet on a sound flange is a fixture matter. Cutting out drain pipe or moving the toilet is not, so call the Inspections Department at 919-463-6190 before work like that.

Do it yourself, or call?

Resetting a toilet on a good flange is a reasonable weekend job. Replacing a flange that is attached to a cast iron or buried line is not, because the cut and the connection must be watertight and set at the right depth.

If the work reveals a damaged drain below the floor, that moves into plumbing installation and repair territory. A sewer smell after the reset can also point to a venting problem, and we cover the fixture side under toilet repair.

Bolts, torque and the small details.

Tighten the closet bolts evenly and stop when the toilet feels snug. Porcelain cracks if it is overtightened. Check the toilet is level in both directions before you cap the bolts.

A plastic bolt cap that sits proud of the porcelain can tell you the bolt is too long, and a hacksaw cut with a clean edge fixes it. A flange with one bolt slot snapped can still hold with a repair ring, but only when the other slots are sound.

Choosing the right flange for the job.

A plain PVC flange is fine for a clean cut-in on plastic drain pipe. A cast iron or brass flange suits a cast iron stack but is heavier and harder to cut. A stainless repair ring is the right call when the flange has cracked but the pipe is solid.

Offset flanges exist for a toilet that needs to shift an inch or two, but they restrict flow slightly and are a last resort. If the toilet is in the wrong place, a plumber can explain whether a shift or a true relocation is the honest answer.

Why the wax ring gets blamed.

Most flange problems get blamed on the wax ring because that is the part people see. A new ring will fail again if the flange is low, loose or tilted. Resetting with the same flange condition just repeats the leak.
