When a plumbing problem is a true emergency.

Most plumbing trouble can wait for a morning appointment, and a few cannot. Water coming through a ceiling, a pipe spraying behind a wall, sewage rising in a tub, or a water heater leaking onto the floor are the situations where an emergency plumber in Morrisville earns the call.

A good rule is to ask whether water is still moving where it should not be. If it is and you cannot stop it at a shutoff, treat it as an emergency. If the leak is a slow drip under a sink and the valve is closed, a same-day appointment when available is usually enough.

Before the plumber arrives.

Four steps that cut the damage.

These apply to almost every plumbing emergency, so learn them before you need them.

Find the main shutoff.

It is usually where the water line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Protect the electrical.

If water touches outlets, appliances or the panel, switch the breaker off from a dry spot.

Stop using drains.

Backed-up water has nowhere to go. Every flush or shower adds to the mess.

Take photos.

Photograph the damage before cleanup. Your insurer will ask for it.

Morrisville water comes from Cary, and that matters at home.

What Morrisville homeowners should know about their water and sewer.

The Town of Morrisville says water and sewer service for its residents is provided by the Town of Cary. Cary draws from Jordan Lake and treats the water at the Cary/Apex Water Treatment Facility. Its 2025 testing summary lists total hardness at about 2 grains per gallon, which Cary classes as moderately soft. For a homeowner that means mineral scale builds slowly, so most water heaters here are not failing from hard-water scale.

Sewer responsibility has a clear dividing line. Cary clears blockages in the main and in the lateral up to the cleanout at the edge of your property. From the cleanout to your house, the owner is responsible. Walk your yard and find the capped pipe now, because a plumber will ask where it is when a drain backs up.

Sources: Town of Morrisville water page, Cary 2025 water quality report, Cary sewer lateral guidance.

A fast-growing town means many owners are new to their plumbing.

Morrisville was chartered in 1875, rechartered in 1947 and grew from about 5,000 residents in 2000 to more than 34,800 by December 2025, according to the Town. About 45 percent of homes are owner-occupied, so many households are renters or recent buyers who have never seen the shutoff valve or the water heater drain.

Check any plumber's licence before you hire.

North Carolina licenses plumbing contractors through the State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors. You can search a licence on the board's public search page at nclicensing.org, and you should do it for any plumber, including us.

The state building code that plumbers work to is the 2024 North Carolina Plumbing Code, mandatory from July 1, 2025, which is built on the 2021 International Plumbing Code with North Carolina amendments. Ask the plumber to confirm it. Ask who pulls the permit when one is needed.
